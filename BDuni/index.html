📖 App Story

A BBA student made a web app? Yep, that’s me. Suddenly, I was thinking about my academic results and how difficult it is to manually track semester performance, required marks, and overall CGPA progress. I felt that having a simple digital platform to store course results and calculate future goals would make student life much easier. Maybe most students usually calculate these things manually. But I wanted to turn this simple idea into a digital solution. So, after spending countless hours learning with Artificial Intelligence, YouTube tutorials, and a lot of experimenting in VS Code, I successfully built it with many bugs 𖢥 Still, I’m really happy that I was able to create something very close to what I imagined.

*By the way, with just one input of your current university results, you can instantly see what your GPA/CGPA would look like at different universities with a single click. It's the kind of thing you can bring up again and again when chatting with your friends. ;P
